**Symptom:** listing text, a review, an owner reply, or a media upload was rejected or removed by moderation.

## Understand the reason

Rejected content comes with a reason tied to platform policy — spam, prohibited content, abusive language, unrelated listing text, or unsupported media. Read the reason first; it tells you exactly what to change. See [content moderation rules](/docs/content-moderation).

## Fix and resubmit

1. Edit the content to comply with the stated reason.
2. Remove anything flagged, such as unsafe links, abusive language, unrelated text, or unsupported media.
3. Save and resubmit. Resubmitted content is re-evaluated.

## If you think it was a mistake

Moderation is partly automated and can occasionally be over-cautious. If your content follows the rules and was still rejected:

- Rephrase to avoid common triggers — too many links, ALL CAPS, or contact details where they aren't allowed.
- If it still won't go through, contact support with the rejection reason and we'll have a person review it.
