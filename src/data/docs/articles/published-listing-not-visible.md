**Symptom:** you published your listing and the dashboard shows it as published, but it still isn't showing in the public directory.

## Checklist

### 1. Confirm it's really *published*

Reopen the listing and check its status. A publish that didn't complete can leave it in **needs completion**. See the [listing lifecycle](/docs/directory-listings).

### 2. Confirm it's complete

Publishing needs every required field. If a required field was later cleared, the listing can drop out of public view. See [listing completeness](/docs/listing-completeness).

### 3. Check your plan

If you have more listings than your plan allows, some stay hidden until you upgrade or remove the extras. See [plans and visibility](/docs/understand-plans-and-visibility).

### 4. Check moderation

Moderation can hold or remove content. If that happened, you'll see a reason. See [my content was rejected](/docs/content-rejected-by-moderation).

### 5. Give it a moment, then refresh

The directory can take a short while to show a newly published listing. Wait a few minutes, then open the public directory in a **private/incognito window** (so you're seeing it as a brand-new visitor, with no old page cached). If it shows there, your earlier view was just out of date.

## Still hidden?

If it's published, complete, within your plan, and not flagged by moderation, contact support with your listing name and we'll look into it.
