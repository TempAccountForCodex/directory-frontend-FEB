This quickstart takes you from an empty account to a published website.

## Before you start

- A verified account. If you don't have one, sign up first.
- A rough idea of which template fits your business — you can change everything later.

## Steps

### 1. Choose a template

From the dashboard, go to **Websites** and choose **Create website**. You'll see templates grouped by category, each with a **Select** button and a **Demo** to preview it in a new tab. Prefer to start clean? Pick the **blank** option. See [use a template](/docs/templates) for how templates work.

### 2. Customize

After selecting, you'll move to a short customize step to set up the basics for your new site. Confirm to create your website.

### 3. Edit your pages

Your site opens in the visual **editor**. Add and arrange **sections**, edit text, and swap images. Your work autosaves as you go. See [build and edit your website](/docs/website-builder).

> Tip: keep your homepage focused — a hero, a short value statement, and a clear call to action go a long way.

### 4. Set your design

In the **Design** tab, pick a theme preset and font and set your brand colors so the site matches your brand. See [themes and branding](/docs/themes).

### 5. Preview and publish

Preview your site (try the desktop, tablet, and mobile views), then **Publish** to make it live at its own web address. Want a branded address like `www.yourbusiness.com`? See [use your own domain](/docs/custom-domain).

## What's next

- Get familiar with the [website workspace](/docs/manage-your-website) — Pages, Media, Domain, SEO, and more.
- Add a [directory listing](/docs/publish-your-first-listing) so people can find you.
- Run through the [launch checklist](/docs/launch-checklist) before you announce.
