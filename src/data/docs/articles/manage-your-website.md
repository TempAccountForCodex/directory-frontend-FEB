Every website has a management workspace where you handle everything beyond editing page content. Open a website from your dashboard to find these tabs.

## The tabs

- **Overview** — a snapshot of your website: status, key details, and quick actions.
- **Pages** — create, open, and organize your pages. Open a page to edit its sections in the [website builder](/docs/website-builder).
- **Design** — your theme: pick a preset (Light, Dark, Colorful, Minimal) and a font, and set your brand colors. See [themes and branding](/docs/themes).
- **Media** — your image library: upload images, reuse them, and copy their links. See [website media library](/docs/website-media-library).
- **Domain** — your web address: copy or change your subdomain, or connect a custom domain. See [use your own domain](/docs/custom-domain).
- **SEO** — titles, descriptions, and settings that control how your site appears in search results.
- **Menus** — build and arrange the navigation menus that link your pages.
- **Forms** — view submissions from contact and signup forms on your site.
- **Integrations** — connect third-party tools and services.
- **Reviews** — see and respond to reviews tied to your business.
- **Analytics** — traffic and engagement for your site.
- **Team** — invite people to help manage the website.
- **Settings** — general website settings.

> Not every tab applies to every plan. Some features depend on your plan — see [understand plans and visibility](/docs/understand-plans-and-visibility).

## A typical workflow

1. **Pages** → open a page and edit its sections.
2. **Design** → set your theme and brand colors.
3. **Media** → upload the images you'll reuse.
4. **Domain** → confirm your address (or connect a custom domain).
5. **SEO** → fill in titles and descriptions.
6. Publish from the editor.
