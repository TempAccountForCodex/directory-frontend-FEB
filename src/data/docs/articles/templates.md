Templates are ready-made website designs. Starting from one saves you time — you get a complete, good-looking layout that you then make your own.

## How templates work

A template is a fully designed website with sample content. When you create a website from a template, you get your own copy of it. From that point it's entirely yours — editing your site never changes the original template, and other people's sites aren't affected by your changes.

## Choosing a template

Browse the available templates from your dashboard or the templates page. **Preview** any template to see the full design with sample content before you commit.

## Making it your own

Once your website is created from a template you can:

- Edit the text and images in any section.
- Add, remove, and reorder sections. See [build and edit your website](/docs/website-builder).
- Apply your brand colors and fonts. See [themes and branding](/docs/themes).

There's no lock-in to the original layout — change as much or as little as you like.
