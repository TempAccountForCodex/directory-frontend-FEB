Guidance for images and media across listings, websites, and stores.

## Supported formats

Use widely supported web formats:

- **JPEG** — photos.
- **PNG** — logos and graphics with transparency.
- **WebP** — smaller files at similar quality.
- **MP4, WebM, or QuickTime** — videos inserted into rich listing descriptions.

Avoid uncommon or very large formats; they may be rejected or load slowly.

## Sizing guidance

| Use | Suggested size |
| --- | --- |
| Logo | Square, ~512×512 |
| Cover / hero | Wide, ~1600×900 |
| Inline listing image | ~1200px on the long edge |
| Inline listing video | Short, compressed clips |

Upload high-quality source images; the platform resizes for display. Keep individual files reasonably small (a few MB at most) so pages stay fast.

## Where media appears publicly

- Listing image shows in directory cards and previews.
- Inline images and videos render inside the full listing description.
- Website images render inside their blocks on the published site.
- Store product images show in the catalog and checkout.

Anything you upload to a **published** item is public — do not upload anything you would not want shown. Text is moderated separately from the visual content inside media files.

## If an upload fails

The usual causes are oversized files, unsupported formats, or backend connectivity. See [upload fails](/docs/upload-fails).
