Run through this checklist before you announce your website or listing. It catches the issues that most often cause a "looks broken on launch day" report.

## Website

- [ ] Homepage hero text and image are final, not placeholder.
- [ ] Every page renders without empty or broken blocks.
- [ ] Theme colors and fonts match your brand.
- [ ] Contact links and forms point to the right destination.
- [ ] The site looks correct on mobile.
- [ ] The site is **published** and loads at its subdomain in a fresh browser session.

## Listing

- [ ] All required fields are filled — [completeness](/docs/listing-completeness) passes.
- [ ] Listing image and any inline description media display correctly.
- [ ] Category and contact details are accurate.
- [ ] The listing is **published** and appears in the public directory.

## Reviews and favorites

- [ ] Submitting a review while logged in works end to end.
- [ ] Favoriting a listing persists after refresh.

## Final verification

- [ ] Open everything in an incognito window — this is what an anonymous visitor sees.
- [ ] Confirm nothing private (drafts, internal notes) is exposed publicly.

If something is visible to you but not to an anonymous visitor, see [why a listing may not show publicly](/docs/why-a-listing-may-not-show).
