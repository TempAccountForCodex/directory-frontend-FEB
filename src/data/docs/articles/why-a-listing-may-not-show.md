If your listing is missing from the public directory, it's almost always one of a few reasons. Check these in order.

## 1. It isn't *published*

A listing is only public when its status is **published**. Drafts, listings that need completion, and archived listings are all hidden. Check the status in your dashboard. See [the listing lifecycle](/docs/directory-listings).

## 2. It isn't complete

If a required field is missing, the listing can't be published (or can't stay published). See [listing completeness](/docs/listing-completeness).

## 3. It's over your plan's limit

If you have more listings than your plan allows, some are hidden until you upgrade or remove the extras. See [plans and visibility](/docs/understand-plans-and-visibility).

## 4. Moderation flagged it

Content that breaks the rules can be held, rejected, or removed. See [content rules and moderation](/docs/content-moderation).

## 5. It just needs a moment

The directory can take a little while to show changes. After publishing, wait a few minutes and refresh — ideally in a private/incognito window so you're not seeing an older cached page.

## Still not showing?

If your listing is published, complete, within your plan, and not flagged, contact support with the listing name and we'll take a look.
