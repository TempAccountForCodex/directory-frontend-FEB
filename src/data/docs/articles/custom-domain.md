Every website gets a free **subdomain** by default (for example `your-business.example.com`). On supported plans you can also connect your own custom domain, like `www.yourbusiness.com`. Both are managed in your website's **Domain** tab.

## Your subdomain

In the **Domain** tab you can:

- **Copy** your subdomain URL to share it.
- **Change** your subdomain to a different name (subject to availability).

This works on every plan — no setup required.

## Connecting a custom domain

A custom domain needs a plan that supports it; if yours doesn't, you'll see a prompt to upgrade. See [understand plans and visibility](/docs/understand-plans-and-visibility).

1. In the **Domain** tab, choose **Add custom domain** and enter your domain.
2. You'll be shown the DNS records to add at your registrar (where you bought the domain) — typically a **CNAME** or **A record**, plus a **TXT record** to verify ownership.
3. Add those exact records at your registrar and save.
4. Come back and confirm. Verification can take a little while as DNS changes spread across the internet.

## After it connects

- Your site becomes reachable at your custom domain.
- Secure browsing (HTTPS) is handled for you once the domain is verified.

## Troubleshooting

- **Not verifying?** Re-check that the CNAME/A and TXT records match exactly what was shown, with no extra spaces.
- **Still showing the old address?** DNS changes can take a few hours — wait and try again.
- If it still doesn't work after a day, contact support with your domain name.
