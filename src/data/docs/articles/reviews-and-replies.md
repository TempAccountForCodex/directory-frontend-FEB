Reviews let visitors share feedback on your listing, and let you respond. Replying shows prospective customers that you're engaged.

## How visitors leave a review

Anyone viewing your listing can write a review, but they must be **logged in** to submit it. The review includes a rating and a comment. If a visitor tries to submit without logging in, they're asked to log in first and then returned to your listing to finish — see [submitting a review sends me to login](/docs/review-submit-redirects-to-login).

## Anonymous reviews

A reviewer can choose to have their review shown **anonymously**. Their name is hidden on your listing, but the review still counts and can be moderated.

## Replying to a review

Open the review from your dashboard and post a reply. Your reply appears under the original review so future visitors can see how you respond to feedback.

A few tips:

- Reply to negative reviews calmly and constructively — other customers are watching.
- Thank people for positive reviews; it encourages more.

## Helpful votes

Visitors can mark reviews as helpful, which surfaces the most useful reviews near the top.

## Moderation

Reviews follow the platform's [content rules](/docs/content-moderation). Abusive or policy-violating reviews can be hidden or removed.
