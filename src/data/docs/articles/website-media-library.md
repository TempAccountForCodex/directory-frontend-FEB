The **Media** tab on your website is a library of every image your site uses, in one place. Open your website and go to **Media**.

## What you'll see

At the top, a few quick metrics:

- **Total Media** — how many images are associated with your site.
- **Pages using media** — how many pages include images.
- **Website Assets** — images set as part of the site itself (logo, favicon, thumbnail, hero banner).

Below that is a searchable gallery of all your images.

## Where images come from

The library brings together two sources automatically:

- **Uploaded assets** — images you upload directly here.
- **Website assets** — images already used across your site, discovered from your pages and sections (and your logo, favicon, thumbnail, and hero banner).

Each item shows its name, where it's used (which pages and sections), and whether it's an uploaded asset or a website asset — so you can tell at a glance what an image is for before changing it.

## Uploading an image

1. Click **Upload** and choose an image file (JPEG, PNG, WebP, and similar).
2. Wait for it to finish — it appears at the top of the gallery.
3. Reuse it anywhere by copying its link (below) or selecting it in a section's image picker.

If an upload fails, see [my photo upload fails](/docs/upload-fails).

## Finding and reusing images

- **Search** by name, page, or section to find an image fast.
- **Copy URL** on any image to grab its link, then paste it wherever you need it.

## Tips

- Upload once, reuse everywhere — there's no need to re-upload the same image per page.
- Keep file sizes reasonable so pages stay fast. See [images and media](/docs/images-and-media) for formats and sizing.
