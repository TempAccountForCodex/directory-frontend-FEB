Moderation keeps the public directory safe and trustworthy. It applies to listing text, reviews, owner replies, and media upload rules.

## What moderation checks

Text content is evaluated against platform policy. The common prohibitions are:

- Illegal content or activity.
- Hateful, harassing, or abusive language.
- Spam, scams, and misleading claims.
- Adult or graphic content where it is not allowed.
- Content that is unrelated to the business, category, or services being listed.

Media uploads are checked for supported file type, size, and unsafe URL schemes. Visual media files are not automatically judged the same way listing text is, so do not upload anything you would not want shown publicly.

## How moderation acts

Depending on the content type and configuration, content may be:

- **Allowed** — published normally.
- **Rejected** — blocked with a reason, so you can fix and resubmit.
- **Removed** — taken down after publication if a violation is found.

## Owner and admin roles

- **Owners** moderate their own listing: replying to or reporting reviews on their listing.
- **Admins** moderate across the whole platform and can override owner decisions.

## What happens to rejected content

Rejected content is not public. You will see the reason and can edit the content to comply, then resubmit. See [content rejected by moderation](/docs/content-rejected-by-moderation).
