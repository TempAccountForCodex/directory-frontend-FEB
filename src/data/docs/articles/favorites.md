Favorites let visitors save listings they like — including yours. Here's how they work, so you know what your visitors experience.

## For signed-in visitors

When a visitor is logged in and favorites your listing, it's saved to their account. Because it's saved to their account, they'll see it on any device they sign in to.

## For visitors who aren't signed in

Visitors don't have to log in to favorite a listing. If they're not signed in, the favorite is saved in their browser instead. That means:

- It stays on that browser between visits.
- It won't follow them to a different device.
- When they log in, account-saved favorites take priority. Browser-only favorites remain local to that browser unless the app imports them.

## Favorites are kept when you archive

If you unpublish or archive your listing and later [republish it](/docs/publish-archive-republish), the visitors who favorited it still have it saved when it returns. Archiving never wipes out favorites.

## What you see

Your listing shows how many people have favorited it, so you can gauge interest over time.
