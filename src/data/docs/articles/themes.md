Your theme controls the overall look of your website — colors and fonts — so the whole site stays consistent without editing each section by hand. Set it in your website's **Design** tab (you can also adjust the theme from within the editor).

## Theme presets

Start from a preset and tweak from there:

- **Light**
- **Dark**
- **Colorful**
- **Minimal**

## Fonts

Choose a font for your site. Available choices include:

- Inter, Roboto, Open Sans, Lato, Poppins (clean sans-serif)
- Playfair Display, Merriweather (classic serif)

Pair at most one or two fonts for a clean, fast-loading site.

## Brand colors

Set your brand colors so buttons, links, and accents match your identity. Every section picks these up automatically, so one change restyles the whole site.

## Tips

- Pick colors with enough contrast for readable text.
- Preview your changes on desktop, tablet, and mobile before publishing.
- Save your changes, then publish to make them live.
