You can update your listing at any time from the website dashboard. Changes you make are saved to the listing and reflected publicly once you save (and, for a new listing, once it's published).

## Fields you can edit

- **Business name** — how your business appears in the directory.
- **Description** — a detailed rich-text description of what you offer. It should be business-relevant and within the required word range, currently 250-2000 words.
- **Category** — choose the category that best fits, so people find you when browsing.
- **Price level** — help visitors understand your typical pricing.
- **Contact details** — email and phone.
- **Location** — full address, city, region, and country.
- **Listing image** — upload a new image, reuse the website image, or remove the selected image.
- **Tags** — short labels that describe your services.

## How to edit

1. Open the listing from your dashboard.
2. Update any field. Required fields are marked — see [listing completeness](/docs/listing-completeness).
3. Save. If the listing is already published, your changes go live after saving.

## Tips for a strong listing

- Write the description for a customer, not a search engine — be clear and specific.
- Pick the most accurate category; the wrong one makes you harder to find.
- Keep contact details current — a dead phone number or link costs you customers.
- Add a strong [listing image or inline media](/docs/add-listing-media) — visual listings get far more attention.
