# Techietribe Pricing Competitor Comparison

Prepared for manager pricing discussion. Prices below are planning recommendations, not implemented production values.

Date reviewed: July 28, 2026

## Manager Feedback Reflected

- Paid users should be able to buy a `1` website plan.
- We should not force users into a 5-website minimum.
- Keep existing plan names:
  - Free
  - Pro
  - Business
- Pricing direction mapped to existing plan names:
  - Pro: `$9/website/month`
  - Early-bird Pro: `$7/website/month`
  - Business: `$17/website/month`
  - Early-bird Business: `$15/website/month`
- Early-bird discounts should be configurable from Super Admin.
- Bulk/volume discount can exist in backend calculation, but should not show as a separate pricing-card chip unless Super Admin enables that display.

## Recommended Techietribe Pricing Model

Techietribe should price paid plans per website because the core value scales with each website and directory listing.

| Package | Standard Price | 1 Website | 5 Websites | Annual With 2 Months Free At 1 Website | Market Position |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Pro | `$9/website/month` | `$9/month` | `$45/month` | `$90/year` | Entry paid website + listing plan |
| Business | `$17/website/month` | `$17/month` | `$85/month` | `$170/year` | Professional growth plan with stronger AI, domains, SEO, analytics and scale |

Early-bird examples:

| Plan | Early-Bird Price | 1 Website | 5 Websites | Annual With 2 Months Free At 1 Website |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Pro | `$7/website/month` | `$7/month` | `$35/month` | `$70/year` |
| Business | `$15/website/month` | `$15/month` | `$75/month` | `$150/year` |

Keep the current planned logic:

- users can start with 1 website
- site-count stops: `1 -> 5 -> 10 -> 20 -> 25 -> 50 -> 100 -> 150 -> 200 -> 250`
- annual billing gives configurable months free
- early-bird, launch, referral and promo rules must be Super Admin-configurable
- backend can calculate volume discount, but visible bulk-discount chip should be optional/configurable

## Why This Pricing Makes Sense

Techietribe is not only competing with website builders. It combines:

- website builder
- AI setup and editing
- business directory listings
- forms and leads
- blogging/content
- SEO
- custom domains
- analytics and growth tools

The new model fixes the biggest manager concern: customers can buy just 1 website, while Techietribe still scales cleanly when they need 5, 10 or more.

## Pro Package Side-by-Side

Recommended Techietribe Pro price: `$9/website/month`, with early bird at `$7/website/month`.

| Product | Market Lane | Price Reference | Website Count / Site Economics | Compared To Techietribe Pro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Techietribe Pro | Website + directory + AI starter | `$9/website/month`; early bird `$7/website/month` | 1 website purchasable; scales by selected site count | Competitive entry paid plan if directory listing and AI starter flow are included |
| Wix Light/Core | Mainstream website builder | Wix official pricing article lists Light around `$17/month` and Core around `$29/month` when billed annually | Wix support says a paid site plan is valid for a single site only | Techietribe Pro is cheaper for 1 site and can include listing/discovery value |
| Squarespace | Mainstream website builder | Official pricing starts around `$19/month` after trial depending plan/billing | Primarily priced per website subscription | Techietribe Pro undercuts Squarespace on entry price |
| 10Web AI Starter | AI WordPress website builder | Official pricing shows Starter around `$10/month` for 1 website | AI WordPress hosting/builder | Techietribe Pro is just below 10Web at `$9`, and early bird is cheaper at `$7` |
| Hostinger Website Builder | Budget website/hosting | Official builder pricing can start around `$2.99/month` promo pricing | Low-cost hosting-led website product | Hostinger can be cheaper; Techietribe should compete on directory discovery, AI setup and business workflow |
| Duda Basic | Agency/multi-site builder | Official pricing lists additional sites around `$19/month` Basic | Extra-site economics are higher | Techietribe Pro is much cheaper for small businesses that need one or a few sites |

### Pro Takeaway

`$9/site/month` is a practical paid entry point for Pro. It is low enough to compete with mainstream builders, but not so low that Techietribe looks like only a hosting product.

Recommended Pro messaging:

> Start with 1 website and directory listing from `$9/month`.

Early-bird messaging:

> Early bird from `$7/month` for your first website.

Recommended Techietribe Business price: `$17/website/month`, with early bird at `$15/website/month`.

| Product | Market Lane | Price Reference | Website Count / Site Economics | Compared To Techietribe Business |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Techietribe Business | Website + directory + AI + growth tools | `$17/website/month`; early bird `$15/website/month` | 1 website purchasable; scales by selected site count | Sits near mainstream single-site builders but adds directory/listing and AI workflow value |
| Wix Core/Business | Mainstream business website builder | Wix official pricing article lists Core around `$29/month`, Business around `$39/month`, and Business Elite much higher when billed annually | Site plan is for one site | Techietribe Business is lower than Wix Core/Business for one site and scales predictably |
| Squarespace Business/Commerce | Mainstream business/ecommerce website | Official pricing starts around `$19/month`, with higher tiers for ecommerce and advanced features | Primarily one website subscription | Techietribe Business is competitive if Business includes domain, SEO, AI, forms and listing value |
| 10Web AI Premium/Ultimate | AI WordPress builder | Official pricing shows Premium around `$15/month` for 2 websites and AI Ultimate around `$23/month` for 4 websites | Can be cheaper per site at higher tiers | 10Web may win on WordPress hosting economics; Techietribe must win on simpler SMB workflow and directory discovery |

### Business Takeaway

`$17/site/month` is defensible for the stronger package. It is still below many mainstream business-site plans while giving Techietribe room to support custom domains, AI usage, SEO, forms, analytics and directory value.

Recommended Business messaging:

> Professional website, AI tools and directory visibility from `$17/month`.

## Recommended Final Package Positioning

| Package | Best Customer | Price Anchor | Primary Value |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Free | New or testing user | `$0` | Launch a simple single-page presence |
| Pro | First paid users, solo operators, creators, local services | `$9/month for 1 website` | Website + directory listing at a low entry price |
| Business | Small businesses that need stronger brand, SEO, AI and growth tools | `$17/month for 1 website` | More professional controls, higher limits and stronger growth features |

## Updated Manager Decision Points

1. Keep existing package names: `Free`, `Pro`, and `Business`.
2. Confirm Pro standard price: recommended `$9/website/month`.
3. Confirm Pro early-bird price: recommended `$7/website/month`.
4. Confirm Business standard price: recommended `$17/website/month`.
5. Confirm Business early-bird price: recommended `$15/website/month`.

## Competitive Risk

Hostinger and some hosting-led products can undercut Techietribe on raw website hosting price. Techietribe should not compete only on hosting price.

Techietribe should compete on:

- 1 website purchasable from the start
- built-in directory discovery
- one listing per website
- AI-assisted setup and editing
- forms, leads and analytics
- simple per-website scaling
- Super Admin-managed early-bird deals, promos, referrals and package rules
