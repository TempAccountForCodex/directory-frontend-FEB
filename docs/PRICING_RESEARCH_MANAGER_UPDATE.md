# Pricing Research Manager Update

Prepared for manager pricing review. This is research and recommendation only; no app code has been changed.

Date reviewed: July 28, 2026

## Manager Question

The current pricing options being compared:

- Option A: Pro `$7/website/month`, early bird `$5/website/month`; Business `$17/website/month`, early bird `$15/website/month`
- Option B: Pro `$9/website/month`, early bird `$7/website/month`; Business `$17/website/month`, early bird `$15/website/month`

Manager instruction:

- Do not force users to buy 5 websites.
- Users must be able to buy 1 website.
- Do not price too high or too low.
- Compare the options deeply before finalizing.

## Short Recommendation

Use Option B:

| Plan | Standard Price | Early-Bird Price | Reason |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Pro | `$9/website/month` | `$7/website/month` | Still below most mainstream website builders, but avoids looking too cheap |
| Business | `$17/website/month` | `$15/website/month` | Matches the market for stronger site features and stays near Duda/Webflow/Wix entry points |

Keep 1 website purchasable.

Keep the site-count stops:

`1 -> 5 -> 10 -> 20 -> 25 -> 50 -> 100 -> 150 -> 200 -> 250`

Do not show a separate bulk/volume discount chip unless Super Admin enables it.

## Why Option B Is Better Than Option A

Option A is more aggressive:

- Pro `$7`
- Early-bird Pro `$5`

That can help early adoption, but it creates three risks:

- It makes Pro look closer to a cheap hosting product than a website + AI + directory product.
- It leaves less room for referral discounts, launch discounts and future promotions.
- It makes it harder to raise price later without frustrating early users.

Option B is more balanced:

- Pro `$9`
- Early-bird Pro `$7`

This still feels affordable, but it is not too low. It is also easier to explain:

> Pro is normally `$9/site`, early birds get it for `$7/site`.

Business at `$17`, early bird `$15`, is reasonable because it sits close to competitor entry-to-mid pricing while Techietribe adds directory/discovery value.

## Competitor Pricing Benchmarks

### Closest Per-Website Competitor: 10Web

10Web is the closest benchmark because it prices AI website plans by website count.

Current official pricing shows:

- AI Starter: `$10/mo`, `$10 per website`, 1 website
- AI Premium: `$15/mo`, `$8 per website`, 2 websites
- AI Ultimate: `$23/mo`, `$6 per website`, 4 websites
- Agency Core: `$112/mo`, `$6 per website`, 20 websites
- Agency Pro: custom, as low as `$2.5/site`

Interpretation:

- Techietribe Pro at `$9/site` is just below 10Web Starter.
- Early-bird Pro at `$7/site` sits between 10Web Premium and Ultimate per-site economics.
- Techietribe Business at `$17/site` must justify itself with directory priority, analytics, collaboration, integrations and stronger business features.

Source: https://10web.io/pricing-platform/

### Duda

Duda separates feature plans from websites and charges extra per site:

- additional Basic sites: `$19/month`
- additional Team/Agency/White Label sites: `$17/month`
- Agency and White Label include 4 sites

Interpretation:

- Techietribe Business at `$17/site` is directly aligned with Duda's extra-site pricing.
- Techietribe Pro at `$9/site` is much cheaper than Duda, which is good for SMBs.

Source: https://www.duda.co/pricing

### Webflow

Webflow's 2026 site-plan update says:

- Basic: `$15/month` yearly or `$25/month` monthly
- CMS/Premium direction: `$25/month` yearly or `$39/month` monthly

Interpretation:

- Techietribe Business early bird at `$15/site` aligns with Webflow Basic annual pricing.
- Techietribe Pro at `$9/site` remains comfortably below Webflow.

Sources:

- https://webflow.com/pricing
- https://help.webflow.com/hc/en-us/articles/51059955082387-Updated-pricing-and-simplified-plans-for-May-2026

### Wix

Wix paid plans are mainly single-site website plans. Wix official FAQ says paid plans are used to connect domains, remove Wix branding and unlock premium features. Current pricing references commonly place Wix around:

- Light around `$17/month`
- Core around `$29/month`
- Business around `$39/month`
- Business Elite much higher

Interpretation:

- Techietribe Pro at `$9/site` is below Wix Light.
- Techietribe Business at `$17/site` is close to Wix Light but should offer directory/listing and AI workflow value.

Sources:

- https://www.wix.com/plans
- https://www.wix.com/blog/wix-premium-plans

### Squarespace

Squarespace pricing is primarily per website and annual billing gives built-in savings. Current market references place entry paid plans around `$16-$19/month`, with business/commerce tiers higher.

Interpretation:

- Techietribe Pro at `$9/site` is below Squarespace entry pricing.
- Techietribe Business at `$17/site` is near entry Squarespace pricing but should include more discovery/AI/business workflow value.

Source: https://www.squarespace.com/pricing

### B12

B12 positions as an AI website plus client engagement platform. Its own pricing copy says higher packages include custom domain, more AI credits, analytics, integrations, conversion tools, payments, invoicing, client intake, email marketing, contact management, contracts and team management.

Interpretation:

- B12 is more expensive and service-business focused.
- Techietribe can undercut B12 while keeping AI + website + business workflow positioning.

Source: https://www.b12.io/pricing/

### Brilliant Directories

Brilliant Directories is directory-first. Current official pricing says plans start from around `$40/month` and include AI tools, hosting, white-label branding, custom domain, SSL and forms.

Interpretation:

- Techietribe Business at `$17/site` is below full directory software.
- Directory/listing value is a key differentiator; we should make it visible.

Source: https://account.brilliantdirectories.com/pricing

## What We Should Do Now

### 1. Recommend This Price Ladder

| Plan | Standard | Early Bird | Notes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Free | `$0` | n/a | Simple one-page/free-template plan |
| Pro | `$9/site/month` | `$7/site/month` | Main paid entry plan |
| Business | `$17/site/month` | `$15/site/month` | Stronger business/growth plan |

### 2. Keep 1 Website Purchasable

Do not present paid plans as starting at 5 websites. The pricing card should allow:

`1 -> 5 -> 10 -> 20 -> 25 -> 50 -> 100 -> 150 -> 200 -> 250`

### 3. Keep Early Bird Clear But Not Overcomplicated

Recommended wording:

- Pro: `Early bird $7/site, normally $9`
- Business: `Early bird $15/site, normally $17`

Avoid stacking too many visible chips. Show annual savings and early-bird savings. Keep volume savings in the detailed breakdown or backend calculation unless Super Admin enables a visible chip.

### 4. Keep All Values Super Admin Configurable

Super Admin must control:

- standard Pro price
- early-bird Pro price
- standard Business price
- early-bird Business price
- early-bird duration
- early-bird eligible plans
- early-bird start/end dates
- annual free months
- volume discount rules
- whether volume discount is visible on cards
- promo/referral percentages
- promo banners

### 5. Do Not Lock Into Permanent Low Pricing

If we want aggressive acquisition, use early-bird rules instead of permanently lowering the standard price.

Recommended early-bird limit options:

- first 100 customers
- first 90 days
- first annual payment
- first billing cycle only

Best recommendation:

> First annual payment or first 90 days, configurable from Super Admin.

## Final Recommendation

Use:

- Pro: `$9/website/month`
- Early-bird Pro: `$7/website/month`
- Business: `$17/website/month`
- Early-bird Business: `$15/website/month`

This is not too high and not too low:

- Pro is below Wix, Squarespace, Webflow and 10Web Starter.
- Early-bird Pro feels meaningfully discounted without devaluing the product.
- Business aligns with Duda's per-site pricing and remains below many business website/tool bundles.
- 1 website remains purchasable, which fixes the main conversion concern.
